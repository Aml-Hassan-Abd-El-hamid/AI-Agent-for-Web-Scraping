Favorite Eminence Products From Maitreyi Ramakrishnan & Other Celeb Stars

Written By

Thea Christie, November 24, 2022

Categories

    Celebrity Skin Care

We’ve been lucky to attract celebrity fans around the world who share their love for Eminence Organic Skin Care. These fans have discovered our products either on their own, via an esthetician or through the on-set makeup artist for their latest TV show or movie. Recently, Never Have I Ever actress Maitreyi Ramakrishnan expressed her love for Eminence products, as did Firefly Lane actors Katherine Heigl and Beau Garrett. Other fans include The White Lotus stars Haley Lu Richardson and Adam DiMarco, who shared praises for their favorite Eminence Organics products. Keep reading to find out which products they love. 

Maitreyi Ramakrishnan

Netflix’s Never Have I Ever is coming up to its third season after extreme success and two seasons of teen heartbreak and adventures. This coming-of-age drama focuses on an Indian-American teenager who wants to improve her high school social status. The lead character, portrayed by Maitreyi Ramakrishnan, faces the hazards of teen life including awkwardness, romantic entanglements and family tensions.

We were excited when Maitreyi Ramakrishnan shared her skin care routine with Vogue, revealing that she pairs the Stone Crop Cleansing Oil and Clear Skin Probiotic Cleanser together for "efficiency sake," and follows up with the Stone Crop Hydrating Mist.

The Stone Crop Cleansing Oil contains stone crop, a hydrating and moisturizing ingredient that revitalizes the look of the skin. This formula also has a Detoxifying Microgreens Complex, a blend of microgreens that protects against the drying effects of environmental stressors and reduces the signs of aging.

The Clear Skin Probiotic Cleanser is ideal for break-out prone skin. This formula contains a Biocomplex2™, a booster of antioxidants that restores the look of radiance and vitality, as well as a cucumber juice that tones and shrinks the appearance of pores. This cleanser moisturizes and nourishes the skin with yogurt and sweet almond milk. 

If you’re dealing with an uneven skin tone, the Stone Crop Hydrating Mist is ideal. This mist contains hydrating and healing stone crop plus lavender, which makes the skin appear calmer and more restored.

Haley Lu Richardson & Adam Di Marco

The White Lotus is an HBO hit that's also topping the charts. This satire follows the lives of hotel employees as well as guests vacationing at the exclusive White Lotus resort and hotel. The show follows many different plot lines simultaneously, from a hotel manager to the life of a young assistant brought along to the resort. 

The young assistant is played by actress Haley Lu Richardson, who became a fan of Eminence after being introduced to the products at a local spa. Richardson shared that after experiencing Eminence products in her routine, her skin became “… so much happier and healthier!” The 27-year-old recently spoke to Vogue, walking them through her skin care and makeup routine. You can view the video below.


﻿The White Lotus actor Adam DiMarco plays a Stanford graduate on a family trip in the drama. A fan of our Coconut Age Corrective Moisturizer, he says, “I was introduced to the Eminence Organics Coconut Age Corrective Moisturizer on set and it’s been the best product ever for my skin! Not only does my skin feel smooth and stay clear, the compliments haven’t stopped since I started using it.” This moisturizer is formulated with coconut oil, a nourishing ingredient rich in antioxidants that moisturizes the skin. It also contains coconut water which provides hydration to the skin. 

Katherine Heigl And Beau Garrett

Netflix hit Firefly Lane is another popular drama that features actors who are Eminence fans. The drama explores the lives of two women and their friendship over the span of their lives, from 1970s to their adulthood in the early 2000s.

Firefly Lane actress Beau Garrett received an Eminence Organics facial, after which she said: "Thank you so much for treating me like a queen!" Her co-star, Emmy award-winning actress Katherine Heigl, has been a loyal Eminence fan for many years: "I can't get enough of the skin care line. I take them on the plane and give myself a facial ..." 

Heigl first came across Eminence Organic Skin Care when visiting a day spa in Utah. She was so pleased with her skin care results that her manager contacted us after the treatment, and our company president, Boldijarre Koronczay, traveled to Katherine’s home to provide a facial and personal skin care consultation.

Do any of these celeb faves pique your interest? Let us know if you’re using any of these products in the comments below or on social media.
