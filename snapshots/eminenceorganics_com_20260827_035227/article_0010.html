Best Skin Care Products According To Celebrities

Written By

Thea Christie, October 13, 2021

Categories

    Celebrity Skin Care

Celebrities like Nicole Kidman and Lori Harvey can spend hours each day in the makeup chair, so they tend to be well versed on the topic of skin care. Plus, being surrounded by a knowledgeable team of makeup artists certainly helps! That’s why we are honored that a star-studded array of fans, including Nicole and Lori, all recently chose to use Eminence Organic Skin Care products. With talents ranging from on-screen accomplishments to Olympic basketball stardom, you'll have to read on for the full list of new Eminence celebrity fans.

Celebrities’ Favorite Skin Care Products

Nicole Kidman

If you’ve been tuning into Amazon Prime’s hit show, "Nine Perfect Strangers," you may be familiar with the enigmatic character Masha Dmitrichenko, played by Nicole Kidman. Masha runs a wellness retreat that lures guests in with the promise of a complete transformation. But as healing begins, the guests begin to doubt the retreat’s unorthodox methods. In an interview with Vogue UK, the show’s lead makeup artist, Nikki Gooley, revealed her secrets to creating Nicole’s beautiful makeup looks. Nikki shares that she “wanted Nicole … to look almost soothing, peaceful, healthy, and radiant, while … having a no-fuss feel.” Nikki achieved a “sheer glow” by using the Bamboo Firming Fluid. She also used a spritz of the Stone Crop Hydrating Mist to “set the look, tone and hydrate the skin.”

Jodie Comer

Another one of Eminence Organics’ fans is Emmy-winning actress and "Killing Eve" star Jodie Comer. Jodie’s latest movie is "The Last Duel," set in 14th-century France, and directed and produced by Ridley Scott himself! The star-studded cast also includes Matt Damon, Ben Affleck and Adam Driver. After Jodie experienced Eminence Organics products on set, she confided to us: "Your products are amazing. The [Clear Skin Probiotic Masque] is life!" 

Ruby Rose

Actress Ruby Rose spoke to Glamour about knowing her way around the skin care world. Ruby says, “I very much believe in skin care. I believe it’s a great way to keep protecting and feeding your skin. And it makes you feel good to do it.”

She added, “I use basically any of the masks from Eminence. I discovered them when I was in Canada, and I’m obsessed with them. I love the Eight Greens Phyto Masque [Hot].”

Sue Bird

Just before the Tokyo Olympics, Eminence Organics received a shout out from basketball star and five-time WNBA champion & Olympian, Sue Bird. In an interview with SELF.com, Sue shared her bedtime routine and her skin care journey.

“Usually my facial care stuff happens right after I shower, which isn’t necessarily at night … I use Eminence Blueberry Soy Night Recovery Cream, but if I’m putting [moisturizer] on earlier in the day, I’ll use something lighter, like Eminence Firm Skin Acai Moisturizer.” 

Ashley Nicole Black

Emmy-award winner Ashley Nicole Black is the star and writer of HBO's comedy "A Black Lady Sketch Show." The multi-talented celebrity shared her skin care routine with Glamour.

“I use the Stone Crop Hydrating Mist. I don’t usually wear makeup if I’m not on camera, so I just mist throughout the day. It provides a great pick-me-up. We would mist all day in the writers’ room on [TV series] 'Bless This Mess.'”

Alexandra Daddario

Actress Alexandra Daddario had her breakout role in "Percy Jackson & the Olympians: The Lightning Thief" in 2010, for which she was nominated for a Teen Choice Award. Most recently, the actress starred in the HBO Max series "The White Lotus."

We were honored when she shared her beauty secrets with Vogue, saying "[the Firm Skin Acai Moisturizer] is one of my favorite all-natural [skin care] products." 

Jessie Mei Li

Jessie Mei Li stars as Alina Starkov in the Netflix fantasy series "Shadow and Bone." She spoke to Byrdie about her role as Alina, her favorite vegan junk food, and her self-care ritual. When asked about her makeup routine, she shared: “I don’t actually wear makeup that often … but I often will just go for a bit of eyelashes and a bit of Eminence Citrus Lip Balm … I try to give my skin a rest.”

Bailee Madison

Bailee Madison has been on-screen for almost her entire life. Most recently, Bailee was the lead in the Netflix musical A Week Away, but you might remember her as an adorable child actress playing May Belle Aarons in the fantasy film Bridge to Terabithia.

Speaking to Byrdie, Bailee was excited to share her new love of skin care. “I have a dear friend … and she’s been helping me with my skin care! She has me on the Eminence [Clear Skin Probiotic] Moisturizer.”

Lori Harvey

Model Lori Harvey, who is also entertainer Steve Harvey’s daughter, spoke to Vogue about her beauty secrets. She enjoys looking at ’90s glam for inspiration when doing her makeup, and uses Eminence Organics products to prep her skin. 

“I have this Eminence Stone Crop Hydrating Mist I like to use as a toner … I’ve always been really obsessed with having … super hydrated and glowy skin.”

“… I scar really easily so this [Bright Skin Licorice Root Booster-Serum] … helps to fade [scars] and keep my skin tone nice and even.”

Whitney Port

TV personality, fashion designer and influencer Whitney Port gave Eminence Organics a shout out on her blog as well as her podcast, With Whit. Whitney shares recommendations from her skin care routine, saying, “… the [product] that I’m loving right now is the Eminence [Facial Recovery Oil]. [It’s] a natural face oil that’s just so luxurious and radiant and I use it at the end [of my regimen] especially when I want even more of a glow …”

Now that you’ve been updated with all the latest celebrity favorites, we hope you’ve been inspired by some of these skin care selections! Let us know if you’re using any of these product favorites in the comments below or on social media.
