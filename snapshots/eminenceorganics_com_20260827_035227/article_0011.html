The Oscars: Skin Care Products To The Stars

Written By

Thea Christie, April 20, 2021

Categories

    Celebrity Skin Care

Daring stories. Dazzling sets. Impeccable costumes, hair and makeup. The Oscars, despite a difficult year, are back to celebrate the best in cinema. The 93rd Academy Awards not only recognizes talented actors. It is also a ceremony honoring artistic and technical professions. Makeup, hairstyling and fashion are under spotlight scrutiny as the world tunes into the event to see the latest in fashion and beauty trends. 

And when it comes to skin care, the trend is to possess a glowing, healthy complexion. That’s why Eminence Organic Skin Care was thrilled to supply products to the set of "Hillbilly Elegy," a movie that is nominated for Best Achievement in Makeup and Hairstyling for 2021. Directed by Ron Howard, the film includes Oscar-nominated actresses Glenn Close and Amy Adams, as well as Freida Pinto, who was already a huge Eminence Organics fan! If you want to see what cleansers, toners, and other products these actresses used on set, keep reading.

 


Hillbilly Elegy: Makeup & Hairstyling

Back in May 2019, when the movie was filming, Eminence Organics received an email from Jamie Hess, one of the makeup artists working with Eryn Krueger Mekash, the film’s makeup department head. Jamie mentioned that “between the glue, prosthetic removers and the weather, keeping everyone’s skin in tip top shape [was] of utmost importance,” explaining that she recently discovered Eminence Organics and was “absolutely in love with it!” After providing some information about the stars’ skin types and concerns, the Eminence Organics team created custom regimens for each star to send to the set. 

"Proper skin prep is invaluable for a good makeup application," Jamie told Eminence Organics. "Since discovering Eminence Organics skincare, I have seen the benefits of these products both personally and professionally and how they can transform the skin. They will remain a staple in my personal and professional make up kit."

Based on the best-selling memoir of the same name, "Hillbilly Elegy" follows J.D. Vance (Gabriel Basso) as he comes of age in an impoverished Ohio town. J.D.’s mom (Amy Adams) falls into a drug-addiction spiral, leaving him in his grandmother's care. Freida Pinto rounds out the ensemble of the Netflix adaptation as the Yale Law School student who dates Vance. 

"Thanks to the talents of Jamie, Eryn, Patricia Dehaney (hair department head) and Matthew Mungle (prosthetic designer), the Fatal Attraction star Glenn is unrecognizable as the family matriarch, Mamaw."

Thanks to the talents of Jamie, Eryn, Patricia Dehaney (hair department head) and Matthew Mungle (prosthetic designer), the "Fatal Attraction" star Glenn is unrecognizable as the family matriarch, Mamaw. Her transformation involved a prosthetic stomach, low-rise jeans, boxy Reebok sneakers, oversize glasses and frizzy dark hair. Similarly, Amy is completely transformed into her character with the help of a team comprising hairstyling, wardrobe and makeup. 

As Eryn describes in her article published on the Make-Up Artists & Hair Stylists Guild blog: “I orchestrated a very precise application and timing with Jamie so that our actors wouldn’t be waiting long for the others to be ready. Glenn’s application started in wig prep with Patti, then she came to us. Jamie applied the right ear and I applied the left. While Jamie painted Glenn’s hands, I started to apply the nose. As soon as I got the nose glued down, Amy would come in for wig prep with Patti.”

When it came time to the transformations on set, makeup artists added a silicone nose and gelatin ears to Glenn’s face as well as age spots, blotchiness, freckles and veins. Despite the less than glamorous transformation, the cast and their complexions were kept healthy with Eminence Organics products. Needless to say, we will be tuning in to see the results of the Oscar winners on Sunday, April 25! 


Unveiling Skin Care Products To The Stars

As Jamie told Eminence Organics: "I am so proud to have been part of the makeup team on "Hillbilly Elegy" and I loved having the Eminence Organics products to use on my talent!" In light of this success, here are some of the most-loved products from celebrities and fans alike. 

Cleansers

Stone Crop Cleansing Oil

Ideal for all skin types, Eminence Organics’ Stone Crop Cleansing Oil is infused with moisturizing oils like organic sunflower and jojoba, which gently dissolves grime, sebum and surface impurities. Cleansing will reveal a beautifully radiant-looking complexion.

Monoi Age Corrective Exfoliating Cleanser

Wash away impurities, remove surface debris and experience smooth skin with Monoi Age Corrective Exfoliating Cleanser. This multi-action exfoliating cleanser is infused with exotic monoi. Monoi is a delicately fragrant Tahitian oil that hydrates and firms the appearance of the skin.

Toners

Stone Crop Hydrating Mist

The Stone Crop Hydrating Mist is a revitalizing and healing toner for all skin types, particularly uneven skin types. It increases your skin’s hydration level and leaves it looking smooth and moisturized. It is cruelty-free and formulated without parabens, sodium lauryl sulfates, animal by-products, synthetic dyes, petrochemicals, phthalates, GMOs and triclosan. 

Neroli Age Corrective Hydrating Mist

A collagen boosting toner with Natural Retinol Alternative and Swiss Green Apple for all skin types, especially mature. The Neroli Age Corrective Hydrating Mist will make your skin feel smoother and help it to appear tighter thanks to the energizing boost of fragrant neroli oil. 

Exfoliants

Strawberry Rhubarb Dermafoliant

Achieve a bright and radiant-looking complexion with our Strawberry Rhubarb Dermafoliant. Rice flour absorbs oil while lactic and salicylic acids remove dead skin cells. The strawberries and rhubarb infuse the skin with antioxidants. 

Mangosteen Daily Resurfacing Concentrate

Use Mangosteen Daily Resurfacing Concentrate to resurface and refine pores for a refreshed appearance. The mangosteen in this leave-on concentrate promotes radiance while the Lactic Acid Complex removes and prevents buildup.  

Serums & Oils

Facial Recovery Oil

Freida Pinto first stopped us in our tracks in the breakout hit Slumdog Millionaire, a role which earned her MTV Movie Award and BAFTA nominations. Interviewed by New York magazine, the actress called out our Facial Recovery Oil as a product that keeps her skin healthy. Freida explains, “This Eminence oil is amazing. I’m constantly getting makeup done, taking make-up off, and rubbing my skin to do so. The Facial Recovery Oil is great for rebalancing my skin and keeping it clear and hydrated.”

Bamboo Firming Fluid

For tighter looking skin, our Bamboo Firming Fluid is the answer. The strengthening agents of bamboo and coconut deeply hydrate with the help of a Natural Retinol Alternative and Swiss Green Apple.

Eye Creams

Bearberry Eye Cream

Rich with antioxidants, the Bearberry Eye Cream contains bioactive ingredients which hydrates the appearance of skin around the eye. Meadow eyebright, hop and bearberry reduce the visible signs of aging. Bearberry Eye Cream is created with Biodynamic® ingredients from Demeter International Certified Biodynamic® farms.

Marine Flower Peptide Eye Cream

Marine Flower Peptide Eye Cream is a hydrating and rich eye cream that minimizes the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles so that your skin maintains long-lasting hydration. Red algae extract is nutrient rich and contains vitamins, minerals and amino-acids to reduce the look of wrinkles and fine lines, while Botanical Hyaluronic Acid from beetroot helps hydration and smooths the appearance of the skin. You can apply this day and night to improve puffiness and the appearance of dark circles around your delicate eye area.

Herbal Eye Make-Up Remover

This pH-balanced make-up remover is formulated with cucumber, lavender, calendula and chamomile to soothe even the most sensitive of eyes. Herbal Eye Make-up Remover contains lavender and chamomile to balance the skin. 

Body Care

Coconut Age Corrective Moisturizer 

If you’re concerned with your skin starting to lose its suppleness, Coconut Age Corrective Moisturizer is designed to help the appearance of your skin instantly tighten and lift. Coconut, shea butter and grape seed oil combine with green apple technology that offers lasting age correction. 

Monoi Age Corrective Night Body Cream

Monoi Age Corrective Night Body Cream is an ultra-rich night body cream that leaves skin looking denser, firmer and smoother. Argan oil is an antioxidant that softens and moisturizes while evening primrose oil and shea butter both moisturize and revitalize the look of the skin.

Oscar-Winning Eminence Organics Fans

In case you needed more recommendations, here are some reviews from other Oscar winners. 

Firstly, Laura Dern, who won an Academy Award for "Marriage Story," is an Eminence Organics fan! Laura considers our Coconut Firming Body Lotion one of her "beloved staples," as shared in an interview with the New York Times.

Laura also told Vogue about the Clear Skin Probiotic Masque: "I love this masque by Eminence ... it is this probiotic masque and it leaves the skin feeling so good ... literally it feels like it's cleaning my skin ... "  

Previous Oscar winner Jennifer Lawrence has her particular Eminence Organics favorites including the Bamboo Firming Fluid, Rosehip Triple C+E Firming Oil and Citrus & Kale Potent C+E Serum. 

We also can’t forget another talented Oscar-winning Actress Marcia Gay Harden who won Best Supporting Actress in the movie "Pollock." We were thrilled when Marcia stopped by our Vancouver office while filming Fifty Shades Darker. Our President and esthetician extraordinaire, Boldijarre Koronczay, gave Marcia a custom Eminence Organics facial using the Bamboo Firming Fluid, Facial Recovery Oil, Firm Skin Acai Cleanser and Monoi Age Corrective Night Cream for Face & Neck. Marcia jokingly asked Boldijarre to marry her immediately, and commented: “… my skin is fabulous! … I have such admiration for all that you're doing.” She quipped: “Boldijarre, you give new meaning to the phrase ‘skin in the game!’”

Who's your favorite Oscar-winning Eminence Organics fan? Let us know in the comments or on social media.
